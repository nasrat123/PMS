## 👨💻 About HMIS

**HMIS (Hospital Management System)** is a Django-based web application designed to manage all aspects of a hospital or clinic efficiently.  
It helps hospitals manage **patients, doctors, appointments, lab examinations, prescriptions, medicines, and finance**.

Key features include:

- Role-based access: **Admin, Reception, Doctor, Lab, Pharmacy, Finance**  
- Patient detail views with history of appointments, lab tests, prescriptions, and invoices  
- Doctor dashboards showing appointments, prescriptions, earnings, and lab requests  
- Finance module with income tracking, expenses, invoices, and running balances  
- PDF/Excel exports for reports and statements  

---

## 🛠️ Features

### Patients Module
- Add, update, view, and delete patient records  
- Detailed patient history  

### Doctors Module
- Add, update, and manage doctor profiles  
- View assigned appointments and earnings  

### Appointments & Lab Examinations
- Schedule appointments  
- Record lab examinations and results  

### Prescriptions & Medicines
- Manage prescriptions and prescribed medicines  
- Track medicine inventory  

### Finance Module
- Income and expense management  
- Auto-calculated running balances  
- PDF/Excel export of statements  
- Dashboard with opening, closing, and net balances
